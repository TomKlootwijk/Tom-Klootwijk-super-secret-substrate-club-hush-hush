# Evidence and Claim Boundary

## Source-derived baseline

UGTS-0 and UGTS-GN 1.1 supply the query-first support, compatibility, guard, event, transition and lineage architecture. UGTS-KC 2.0 supplies the parameterized pattern, field, topology, kinematic and bounded-dynamics layer.

## Engineering expansion

Mechanisms M258-M329 are explicitly labeled 3.0 engineering additions. They are not represented as if they were all present in the source PDFs.

## What the package establishes

- The dependency-free reference modules execute the included vertical slice.
- 117 unit tests pass in the captured environment.
- The demo produces a scene, two-hand transform event, replay log, SVG previews, glTF JSON and USDA text.
- Catalog IDs and counts are machine checked.

## What the package does not establish

- A physical-GPU rendering or compute advantage.
- Full Vulkan, WebGPU, OpenXR, OpenUSD, MaterialX or OpenColorIO conformance.
- Production collision, skeletal animation, character physics or multiplayer infrastructure.
- Universal constant-time, zero-memory, zero-latency, zero-heat or one-bit-complete-state claims.
- Legal authorship, identity or intellectual-property status.
