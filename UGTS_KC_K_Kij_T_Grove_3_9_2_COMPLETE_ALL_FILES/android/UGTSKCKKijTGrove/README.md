# UGTS-KC 3.9.2 — K-Kij-T / Grove

Native Android 3D runtime.

Focus: Mali-G720 MC7 / POCO X7 Pro 12 GB juiciness with general Android fallback tiers.

Mobile controls: drag on the left side to move and tap there to jump; drag on the right side to look
and tap there to dash. Pointer-ID tracking keeps movement active during a second-finger dash and keeps
pinch zoom stable. Keyboard uses WASD/arrows, J for jump, Enter/Shift for dash, and Space for both
beginner actions. Gamepad A jumps and B dashes.
