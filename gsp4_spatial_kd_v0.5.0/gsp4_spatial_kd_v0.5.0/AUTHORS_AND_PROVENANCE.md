## Engineering extension

GSP4 Spatial Knowledge Distillation v0.5.0 is an engineering implementation produced from the supplied architecture and request. Its role is to make the following concepts executable:

- query-first support, compatibility, guard, event, transition, lineage, and novelty;
- coordinates separated from durable identity;
- deterministic seed/grammar state separated from exogenous novelty;
- sparse variable-length observations rather than padded frames;
- offline large-model knowledge transfer into an online compact GNN;
- direct UGTS ABI export for GPU-native execution.
