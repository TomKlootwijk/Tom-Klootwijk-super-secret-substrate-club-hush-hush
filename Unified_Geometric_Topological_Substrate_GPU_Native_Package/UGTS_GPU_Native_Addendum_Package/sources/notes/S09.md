# S09 — Radix, active bits, Pascal parity and fractal motifs

## Source-derived mechanisms
Radix thresholds at powers of the base; decimal 9→10 and binary 1→10; `19₁₀ = 10011₂`; removing zero/inactive positions leaves three active bits; mapping three active positions to three syllabic markers; Pascal-triangle parity produces Sierpiński structure; powers of two define scale boundaries; lemniscate crossing/chirality, cone and log-polar motifs.

## Technical normalization
The phonetic mapping is metadata attached to Hamming weight, not numerical equality. Pascal parity and Sierpiński generation are exact and GPU-friendly. Later claims about Noether, von Neumann bypass and perfect phi circulation are retained only as prompts or rejected where unsupported.
