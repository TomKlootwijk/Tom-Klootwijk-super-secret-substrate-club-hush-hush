# Tom Klootwijk Signature Edition

“Signature Edition” is the release/edition label requested for this archive. It records
Tom Klootwijk as the requested author/edition attribution and preserves the earlier requester-
supplied Kees Klootwijk substrate attribution.

It is not a cryptographic signature, government identity check, proof of ownership, legal
certification or transfer of third-party rights. The release manifest and SHA-256 checksums prove
file integrity only.
