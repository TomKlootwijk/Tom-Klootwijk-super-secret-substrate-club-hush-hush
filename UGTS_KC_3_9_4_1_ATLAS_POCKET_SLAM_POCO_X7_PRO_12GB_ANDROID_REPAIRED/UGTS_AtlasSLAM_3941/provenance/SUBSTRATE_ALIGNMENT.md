# Substrate alignment

This implementation follows the project source line rather than treating a mesh or preview as authority.

- UGTS-KC 2.0 supplies the typed pattern/field, SE(3) kinematic, support, compatibility, guard, event, transition, and lineage discipline.
- KC Two Hands 3.0 supplies stable scene identity, proposal verification, deterministic ordering/conflict policy, replay/checkpoint thinking, conventional spatial indexing, and separate geometry-error roles.
- UGTS-KC 3.6 supplies content-addressed definitions and explicit operation order.
- SCLP 3.6.2 supplies the finite-packing/error-boundary discipline: packed fields are useful only with declared ranges and do not imply infinite detail or semantic equivalence.
- KC Atlas 3.9.3 supplies the spatial-evidence ledger and frame-observation bridge targeted by this Android shell.
- The supplied Grove 3.9.2 Android package was used as the practical native-build/device-profile baseline; it is referenced by hash rather than nested again.

The Android layer captures observations and display state. The platform-independent `SlamEngine` remains the map/event authority.
